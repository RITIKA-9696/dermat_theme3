Our Philosophy

A quiet studio built on genuine care.

"We never wanted to be the busiest clinic in the city — only the calmest one on the street."

Serenity Skin Spa began in 2014 with a single chair and a simple belief — that advanced dermatology should feel as restorative as the best spa. We take fewer patients each day so every visit feels personal, unhurried, and genuinely calm.

Our team combines board-certified dermatologists with medical-grade technology, and delivers everything in an atmosphere designed to help you exhale.

Common Questions

Things patients often ask.

A few answers before your first visit — and if your question isn't here, we're always happy to talk it through.

Call the Studio
Which treatment should I start with?

Almost always, a consultation. We'll examine, listen and recommend a plan in order of priority — sometimes starting with something simple and gentle before more advanced treatments.

Are laser treatments painful?

Most patients describe laser treatments as a brief warming or snapping sensation. We use numbing cream wherever needed and adjust energy levels to your comfort throughout.

How long until I see results?

Hydrafacials: immediate. Injectables: 2–4 weeks. Lasers: progressive over 4–8 weeks. Acne therapy: 3–6 months. We'll give you a realistic timeline at your consultation.

Do you treat children?

Yes — we offer pediatric dermatology for common concerns like eczema, birthmarks and skin infections, delivered in a child-friendly way.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — for larger treatment plans we offer bundled pricing and interest-free instalments. Ask during your consultation.

Can I combine treatments?

Often yes — many patients combine treatments for better results. We'll plan a combined protocol with appropriate spacing so your skin recovers properly between sessions.
